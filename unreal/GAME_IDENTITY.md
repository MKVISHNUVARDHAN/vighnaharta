# What this game is

**Vighnaharta: Path of Light** is one short 3D arcade run, not six minigames.

You are **Mooshak**, Ganesha’s mouse. The Rath is already moving down the **center of the festival street**. You run ahead and clear the road with your **tail**.

## Must do (fail if you don’t)

1. Snap every **RED GATE** on the center line before the Rath reaches it.
2. Reach the **ghat**.
3. Keep at least one **bell**. Three ceremonial stops = lose.

## May do (fun, not fail)

Swing gold balls. Surf brown carts. Yank orange baskets. Take the left mouse shortcut.

## Feel order

Fun (Tail toys) → Tension (Rath distance is real) → Panic (5m, skipped gate) → Heroic win (Path of Light + music swell).

Sound drives the panic: distant tabla when safe, crowd and bells when the Rath is close. The camera is cinematic (16:9, lag, intro look at the Rath) but never fights steering.

## Asset rule

Only CC0 / already-in-repo packs: KayKit city bits, Kenney City Kit Commercial, Kenney Nature, Kenney RPG/Impact/Jingles, and the festival recordings already in `public/assets/audio/festival`. No paid Indian stall packs. No random Sketchfab with unclear licenses.
